# Scorecard

Score each dimension 1 to 4 on evidence from the exercises. Write the evidence before the debrief discussion.

Hire at a weighted average of 3 or above, with no dimension at 1. A 1 on verification mindset is a no, however good the rest.
Double-weighted: verification mindset, spec writing, regulatory domain sense, platform and resilience.

| Dimension | 1: Concern | 2: Developing | 3: Solid | 4: Exceptional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Requirement clarity | Starts building from the vague brief | Asks a few obvious questions | Asks risk-ranked questions on time, scope and definitions | Finds ambiguities the interviewer hadn't seen |
| Spec writing | Prose with no testable criteria | Some criteria, edge cases missing | Testable criteria, edge cases, clear what/how split | Spec an agent could implement with no further input |
| Verification mindset | Relies on the agent's own tests | Adds unit tests | Independent tests plus reconciliation to source | Designs controls and monitoring for ongoing correctness |
| Code review | Misses the seeded flaws | Finds one or two, mostly style | Finds four or more and traces them to the spec | Also proposes systemic prevention |
| Regulatory domain sense | Treats reports as generic data feeds | Knows terms, not consequences | Understands amendments, lineage, audit and rejections | Reasons about regulator expectations and risk |
| Platform and resilience | Ignores failure modes | Names tools, not trade-offs | Designs for failure, duplicates, recovery and SLA | Backs it with numbers, drills and real incident experience |
| AI-assisted workflow judgment | Blind trust or blanket rejection | Uses tools without clear boundaries | Clear view of what to delegate, review and never delegate | Has shaped team practice and can teach it |
| Communication and ownership | Defensive, vague about failures | Describes events, not lessons | Owns mistakes, explains clearly to non-engineers | Influences stakeholders and resolves conflict |
| Seniority and leadership | Executes tasks only | Mentors informally | Leads delivery and raises the team's bar | Sets direction across teams |
