# Interview flow

Five stages, about 3.5 hours of candidate time. Each stage has a different interviewer so no single score carries the decision.

| Stage | Length | Interviewer | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Screen call | 30 min | Hiring manager | Motivation, seniority, regulatory exposure, AI tooling experience |
| 2. Spec-writing work sample | 60 min | Senior engineer | Requirement clarity, spec writing, verification mindset |
| 3. Code review and platform design | 45 min | Peer engineer | Review skill, delegation judgment, platform and resilience |
| 4. Domain and stakeholder conversation | 45 min | Compliance, ops or reporting analyst | Regulatory sense, communication with non-engineers |
| 5. Behavioural and values | 30 min | Hiring manager or skip-level | Ownership, handling incidents, leadership |

## Running it well

- Share the format and the tools in advance. Don't test whether they can guess your process.
- Use the same exercises and the same rubric for every candidate.
- Leave 5 minutes at the end of every stage for the candidate's questions, and note what they ask.
- In the work sample, the clarifying questions and the spec are written by hand with no AI. AI is allowed only for the last 20 minutes, so tell candidates in advance. Score the questions they ask and the corrections they make to the AI output.
